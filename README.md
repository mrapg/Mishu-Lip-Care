# Mishu Lip Care 💋

A cute, block-style Progressive Web App (PWA) designed with a 3D Voxel Vaseline render, customizable reminders, rotating cute messages, and real-time partner tap vibration!

---

## ✨ Features

- **🫧 3D Voxel Vaseline Animation**: Smooth floating and hovering 3D voxel jar with sparkle effects and interactive tap squish-bounce.
- **📳 Real-Time Partner Vibration Sync**:
  - Tap the 3D jar on your phone to physically vibrate Mishu's phone in real time!
  - Cross-phone communication powered by secure WebSocket relay.
  - Generates tactile sub-bass haptic pulses that vibrate even on iOS devices.
- **💋 16 Personalized Cute Messages**: Fisher-Yates shuffled romantic reminders dedicated to Mishu.
- **⏰ Flexible Reminder Timer**: Presets for 15, 30, 45, 60 minutes or any custom interval with countdown progress bar.
- **📱 PWA & iOS Home Screen Ready**:
  - Fullscreen standalone app experience on iPhone.
  - Custom 3D Voxel Vaseline app icon.
  - Offline support via Service Worker.

---

## 🚀 How Mishu Installs It on iPhone

1. Open the live link in **Safari** on iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button (`↑`).
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. The **MISHU** Vaseline icon appears right on her phone screen! 🎉
